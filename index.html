About

Technical enough to challenge the plan. Commercial enough to grow the account.

I am the primary point of contact for technology delivery and business growth with C-level executives and senior stakeholders at major European corporations. My work is to translate complex technical solutions into business value, and to keep strategic, operational and tactical governance running while the programme moves.

I have managed multi-million euro accounts with distributed teams of 40+ professionals across 7 countries, delivering core software platforms and large-scale digital transformation across EMEA. I influence at every level, from technical teams to the board, so that service delivery and revenue growth stay aligned.

I also teach. Since 2010 I have co-taught Project Management to the Master in International Management at ESADE Business & Law School.
